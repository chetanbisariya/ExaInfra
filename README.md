**ExaInfra.in** is India's sovereign-standard legal due diligence, commercial real estate (CRE) yield simulation, and statutory asset verification terminal. Engineered to provide institutional-grade title screening, CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR 2026) construction costing, and high-stakes property transaction verification conforming to Indian statutory frameworks.

---

## 🏛️ Core Terminal Architecture (4 Pillars)

### 1. 🛡️ Property Legal Scam Radar & Title Due Diligence
- **30-Year Encumbrance Audit**: Cross-references Sub-Registrar Form 15 & Form 16 encumbrance certificates.
- **BBMP A-Khata vs B-Khata Verification**: Resolves revenue extract legitimacy, betterment charge clearance, and DC conversion validity under Section 95 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act.
- **BDA Rajakaluve & NGT Lake Buffer Scanner**: Real-time screening against National Green Tribunal (NGT) 50-meter primary stormwater drain buffers and 75-meter lake perimeter boundaries to protect buyers against demolition risk.
- **Section 65B Certificate**: Generates court-admissible electronic evidence certificates under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 & Information Technology Act 2000.

### 2. 🏢 Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Pre-Leased Yield Engine
- **Cap Rate & IRR Simulator**: Models gross and net yields (8.8% to 10.4%) across Grade-A IT parks, pre-leased PSU/private bank branches, and institutional retail assets.
- **15-Year Cash Flow Projection**: Compounding 4–5% annual lease escalations, weighted average lease expiry (WALE), and lock-in expiration risk modeling.
- **Tax Optimization**: Computes Section 24 standard 30% statutory deductions, property tax outgo, and post-tax institutional distributions.

### 3. 🏗️ Construction Cost BoQ & Contractor Escrow Ledger
- **CPWD DSR 2026 & NBC 2016 Compliant**: Granular Bill of Quantities (BoQ) for residential, villa, and commercial structures across Standard (₹1,950/sq ft), Premium (₹2,650/sq ft), and Ultra-Luxury (₹3,850/sq ft) specifications.
- **Milestone Escrow Framework**: RERA-compliant 5-stage tranche disbursement system tying contractor payments to verified structural engineering milestones.

### 4. ⚙️ Institutional Real Estate & Statutory Asset Tools
- **Guidance Value & Stamp Duty Engine**: Sub-Registrar guidance rate verification with Section 50C deemed consideration valuation.
- **Section 54 Capital Gains Shield**: Budget 2024 LTCG tax optimization model (12.5% without indexation vs 20% with indexation) with Section 54EC bond allocation and Capital Gains Account Scheme (CGAS) timelines.
- **Location-Wise Municipal Property Tax Engine**: Statutory property tax model across BBMP Bengaluru (UAV SAS Scheme), BMC Mumbai (Capital Value System with <500 sq ft flat exemption), MCD Delhi (Unit Area System Categories A-H), GHMC Hyderabad (ARV with 33.33% maintenance deduction), GCC Chennai (Revised Street Rate), and PMC Pune (40% PT40 residential self-occupied concession) with early rebate optimization and direct official municipal portal links.
- **RERA Delay Penalty Claim Calculator**: RERA Section 18 statutory claim model at SBI MCLR + 2% compounding interest.
- **SM REIT vs Direct CRE Modeler**: SEBI SM REIT Regulations 2024 yield, liquidity, and tax comparison.
- **FAR / FSI & NBC Density Modeler**: National Building Code 2016 setbacks, road width multipliers, and premium FAR calculations.
- **NRI Property Sale TDS (Section 195)**: Foreign remittance withholding tax optimizer with Section 197 Lower TDS Certificate (Form 13) guidance.

- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and Modern ES6+ JavaScript (Zero heavyweight dependencies, instant page load speeds).
- **Design System**: Sovereign institutional theme — Crisp slate surfaces, Navy authority accents (`#003366`), and Bharat saffron highlights (`#E05624`).
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (Headings), Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface), JetBrains Mono (Cryptographic proofs & numeric data).
- **Backend API**: PHP 8.x with SQLite / PDO database support for contact transmissions and enterprise retainers.
- **Compliance**: Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and ultra-wide displays; WCAG 2.1 AA accessible; zero-dependency architecture.

## 🚀 Local Development Setup

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/chetanbisariya/ExaInfra.git
   cd ExaInfra
   ```

2. **Serve locally**:
   ```bash
   # Using Python 3
   python3 -m http.server 8091

   # Or using PHP built-in server
   php -S 127.0.0.1:8091
   ```

3. **Open in browser**:
   Navigate to [http://127.0.0.1:8091](http://127.0.0.1:8091).
