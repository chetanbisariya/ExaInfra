⚖️ SECTION 45(5A) TAX DEFERRAL SAFEGUARD:

Under Section 45(5A) of the Income Tax Act, capital gains tax liability does NOT arise upon signing the JDA or handing over land possession. It is legally deferred to the financial year in which the Certificate of Completion (CC) is issued by the municipal planning authority. Note: The landowner must not alienate or transfer their share prior to CC issuance.
